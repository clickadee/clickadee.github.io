About

Hi! I'm Lucia, the founder and teacher behind Clickadee.

I'm currently a PhD student at Harvard, where I research AI for medicine, with a focus on computer vision for 3D medical imaging. Before graduate school, I studied Computer Science at Stanford, where I completed both my B.S. and M.S. degrees.

But outside of research, I love teaching.

I began working as a tutor when I was 15, and since then I have taught computer science in classrooms across the globe — from "hello, world!" to self-driving cars. I have served as a teaching assistant under some of the world's best faculty, and as a lecturer leading my own courses. Over the years, I've also taught for several online coding and AI schools. I loved working with students, but I often found myself wishing the learning experience could be different.

I kept coming back to the same questions:

Why do we always use the simplest datasets available?

Why do we treat programming errors like a "wrong answer" when debugging is one of the most important parts of being a programmer?

Why spend so long on isolated programming exercises before students get to the ideas that made them excited about computer science in the first place?

And perhaps most importantly:

What if learning AI felt less like taking a class and more like joining a research lab?

That's the idea behind Clickadee.

I want students to learn the fundamentals, but I also want them to use those fundamentals to build things that are uniquely theirs. I want them to get comfortable being stuck, asking questions, experimenting, and figuring things out.

For the founding cohort, I'll personally teach every class and mentor every student. There will be just 15 students.

I'm hoping to build Clickadee into a place where students can keep learning and building together over time, with follow-up courses in 2027 and more cohorts after that. But for now, I'm starting with 15 students and building from there.

If this sounds like the right fit for your student, I'd love to hear from them! You can apply here.

And if you'd like to follow along as I build Clickadee, you can find the story behind the process on my Substack.
