Cohort 1.0

10 weeks. One research project, built from the ground up.

Each week mixes lectures, hands-on coding, guest speakers, and paper discussions, all in service of the independent research project you'll bring to Demo Day. Tap any card below for the full weekly rundown.

WEEK 01

Welcome to the Lab

  • Meet your cohort
  • Colab & environment setup
  • Build your first model: linear regression

WEEK 01 — SAT SEP 19

Welcome to the Lab

We will get to know each other with some community-building activities and go over the logistics of the course. Students will learn the basics of linear regression. After getting set up in Google Colab, students will build their very first machine learning model.

Lab Meeting

  • Walkthrough of the research project ahead, including examples
  • How to use your research notebook

WEEK 02

How Models Learn

  • Gradient descent
  • Dataset splits
  • Overfitting

WEEK 02 — SAT SEP 26

How Models Learn

We will take a closer look at what's happening inside a linear regression model and learn about the logic of gradient descent. Then, we will discuss how to split a dataset into training, validation, and testing splits, and learn about data leakage. We will discover what overfitting means.

Lab Meeting

  • Guest researcher presentation
  • How to read a research paper

WEEK 03

Making Decisions with Data

  • Logistic regression
  • Evaluating binary classifiers

WEEK 03 — SAT OCT 3

Making Decisions with Data

Students move from predicting numbers with linear regression to predicting categories with logistic regression. We will learn how to evaluate binary classifiers in real-world situations, including confusion matrices and AUROC. We will also introduce multi-class classification and softmax.

Lab Meeting

  • Students give lightning talks on a research paper they read

WEEK 04

The Messy Side of Data

  • Finding structure with unsupervised learning
  • Cleaning messy, real-world data
  • Choosing your research question

WEEK 04 — SAT OCT 10

The Messy Side of Data

We will learn two skills every researcher needs: finding patterns in unlabeled data (clustering, dimensionality reduction), and wrangling real, messy datasets with pandas and NumPy.

Lab Meeting

  • How to formulate a strong research question
  • Setting up your research project in Colab
  • How to effectively use AI coding assistants as a beginner researcher

WEEK 05

Building Your First Neural Network

  • Neurons, layers & activation functions
  • Backpropagation
  • Training your first network in PyTorch

WEEK 05 — SAT OCT 17

Building Your First Neural Network

We will learn the anatomy of a neural network (neurons, layers, and activation functions) and how neural networks learn through backpropagation. Then, students will build their first neural network from the ground up using PyTorch.

Lab Meeting

  • Share your research project idea in small groups
  • Work on your research project (office hours)

WEEK 06

Tuning the Machine

  • Regularization
  • Hyperparameter tuning
  • More PyTorch practice

WEEK 06 — SAT OCT 24

Tuning the Machine

We will take a deeper dive into neural networks and get more practice using PyTorch. We will learn how to prevent a model from memorizing its training data, and how to make intentional choices about architecture and hyperparameters instead of guessing.

Lab Meeting

  • Guest researcher presentation
  • Work on your research project (office hours)

WEEK 07

Teaching Computers to See

  • Convolutional neural networks (CNNs)
  • Convolutions & pooling
  • Applying a CNN to your own data

WEEK 07 — SAT OCT 31

Teaching Computers to See

We will learn about how networks can process images using convolutions, pooling, and convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Then, we will experiment with building and applying a CNN to a real dataset.

Lab Meeting

  • Scientific writing tips
  • Starting your research blog post
  • Work on your research project (office hours)

WEEK 08

How Transformers Work

  • Transformers & attention
  • Why transformers replaced older architectures
  • Applying a transformer in practice

WEEK 08 — SAT NOV 7

How Transformers Work

We will learn the architecture behind today's most powerful AI models: transformers! We will discuss attention and students will implement the attention mechanism with a small example. Then, using a pretrained transformer, we will visualize real attention patterns on our own sentences.

Lab Meeting

  • What makes a good figure
  • Work on your research project (office hours)

WEEK 09

Language Models

  • How large language models are trained
  • Prompting & fine-tuning

WEEK 09 — SAT NOV 14

Language Models

We will explore how LLMs work under the hood, including training, prompting, and fine-tuning.

No lab meeting this week. Instead, students will meet with me in 1:1 sessions to discuss their research project.

WEEK 10

Reinforcement Learning

  • Reinforcement learning & world models
  • Research project work time
  • Polishing your presentation

WEEK 10 — SAT NOV 21

Reinforcement Learning

We will cover our last technical topics: reinforcement learning and world models. Then, we will have dedicated time for students to wrap up their research project and work on their Demo Day presentations.

No lab meeting this week. Instead, students will meet with me in 1:1 sessions to discuss their research project.

DEMO DAY

Sat, Dec 5

  • Student project presentations
  • Open to family & friends
  • Celebrating the cohort

DEMO DAY — SAT DEC 5

Sat, Dec 5

Students present the research projects they've built over the last ten weeks to the rest of the lab, family, and friends!
